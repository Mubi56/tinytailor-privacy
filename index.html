Privacy Policy for Tiny Tailor

Last updated: 2 November 2026

App: Tiny Tailor (com.tinytailor.game)

Developer: TinyTailor

Contact: mubashariqbalkhan1@gmail.com

The short version

Tiny Tailor does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or contact details. Your game progress is stored only on your device. Depending on the store you installed the game from, it may show ads or offer optional purchases, and the companies that provide those services handle some data as described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels finished, stars, coins, shop upgrades, thread colour, settings, and whether you removed ads) is saved in a file on your device. We do not receive it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

Ads

Some versions of Tiny Tailor show rewarded ads (for hints and bonus coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels, using Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service from Unity. Whether ads appear depends on the version you installed. If your version shows no ads, none of the ad data below is collected.

When ads are shown, LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect and use, for example:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and performance data.

These companies act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Purchases

Where the game offers optional purchases (for example, a "Remove Ads" item on Google Play), they are handled entirely by the store you bought from. We do not see or store your payment details. We are told only that a purchase of a particular item was completed, so we can unlock it. Some versions of the game, such as the one on Amazon Appstore, offer no real-money purchases.

Children

Tiny Tailor is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device's settings.
  • Where offered, remove ads by buying Remove Ads in the app.
  • Delete all data held by the app by uninstalling it.

Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. Data held by the third parties above is kept according to their policies. Data sent to them travels over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
